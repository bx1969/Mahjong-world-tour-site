Mahjong World Tour

Match tiles. Tour the world.

A relaxing tile-matching game where every board tours a famous sight in one of 100 great cities. Match pairs, piece together the sight's photo, and fill your passport with stamps.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email brandon.holbrookii@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Common questions

Does my progress sync between devices? Not yet. Progress is saved on your iPhone only.

How do I appear on the leaderboards? Sign in to Game Center in the iPhone Settings app. Your miles post automatically while you play.

Where do the photos come from? Wikimedia Commons. Every photographer and license is listed in the game under Lounge → Settings → Credits & licenses.
