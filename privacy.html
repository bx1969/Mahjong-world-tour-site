Privacy Policy

Mahjong World Tour · Effective October 23, 2026

Mahjong World Tour is a tile-matching game. We built it to collect as little as possible: the game runs on your device, we don't run accounts or analytics, and we don't have servers that receive your data. The game shows occasional ads from Google AdMob, described below.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress (miles, cities visited, passport stamps, settings) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Your home airport. You pick it from a list, or tap "Use my location" to find the nearest one. If you use your location, the iPhone asks your permission first; the location is used once, on your device, and only the airport code is saved.
  • Your passport name and portrait. If you design a portrait, only your choices (skin tone, hair and so on) are saved, on your device. If you use a photo, the game turns it into a painted portrait right on your device. The photo is never uploaded or sent anywhere, and the game only keeps the painted portrait.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, the game sends three numbers to Apple's Game Center leaderboards: miles earned today, miles earned this week, and how many cities you've stamped. Apple shows these with your Game Center nickname to other players. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; you can turn it off in the iPhone Settings app under Game Center.

Ads

Between some sights the game shows a full-screen ad from Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when the iPhone asks), your approximate location based on your IP address, and information about ad views, taps and app performance. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the game asks for your consent first. You can turn off tracking at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking. Learn how Google uses this information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

What we collect

We, the developer, don't collect or receive any of your data. Apart from Google's ads and Apple's Game Center described above, nothing leaves your device.

Links

The Credits screen links to photo pages on Wikimedia Commons. Those open in your browser and are covered by Wikimedia's privacy policy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. If we ever add features that collect data (for example, online accounts or ads), we'll describe them here first and ask for any permissions the iPhone requires.
